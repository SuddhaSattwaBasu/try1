Career Story

From patent examiner's desk to AI governance board — the long version.

I started in intellectual property, doing the unglamorous groundwork of freedom-to-operate searches and inventor interviews for industrial R&D teams. Nineteen years on, that same discipline — find the defensible position, argue it, ship it — now shows up in AI governance memos, Agentic AI roadmaps, and enterprise data platforms for Fortune 100 clients. The thread runs through a founded startup at UC Berkeley SkyDeck, a $40M prop-tech platform, an AI diagnostics patent estate, and a Bengaluru AI product hub built from nothing.
